### `> whoami`

I spent **2+ years on the other side of the ticket queue** — resolving escalations for a global e‑commerce seller-support operation. I know what customers actually ask, where support workflows break, and what "good enough" costs at scale.

Now I build the systems that fix it: **retrieval-augmented assistants that cite their sources, multi-agent triage pipelines, and low-code automations** that remove the busywork.
